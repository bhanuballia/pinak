Leo is a fixed sign so it shows the individual cannot change his mind quickly. The decision of the individual cannot change easily. Moon shows the mood of individual because Moon is the Karaka of Mann. Leo is the fifth sign of the zodiac. It is a fiery sign and masculine gender. It shows creativity and intelligence. Sun and Moon are friends in Vedic astrology. But both have different nature.

Moon in Leo shows the individual’s emotional nature with parents, especially with mother. You want mother’s care and attention all the time. You may want to spend more time with your mother. You are likely very popular among people and quite possessive about your image. You have a leadership quality to support others materially and emotionally with heart.

You have strong intuitive power. You are likely bossy with your brothers, sisters, and friends. Sometimes you may show laziness in work. You flow with emotions and get influenced by others quickly. You may spend money on personality development. You will be quite emotionally attached to your life partner. You give love and respect to your life partner and in return expect the same.


Effects of all houses with Moon in Leo

Moon in Leo in 1st House
Moon in the first house in Leo represents the loving and charming personality of individual. You have a natural attraction in the crowd. You have a talent for different arts and are very kind and want personal freedom. You are emotional and sensitive. You are short tempered and easily get angry.

With an assertive attitude, you have high ambition to achieve success. You are kind and believe in charity. You want to enjoy a luxurious life. You spend money on comforts and luxury items. You may live in a foreign country with your mother. You may give or spend your money in nurturing needy people. It is seen that your mother impacts your personality.


Moon in Leo in 2nd House
Moon in the second house in Leo represents an attractive and charming personality with a skillful voice. In your voice, an emotional and sensitive tone is seen by others. You show respect to others in your speech. Your family has a good name, fame and wealth. You may be born in a reputative family.

You are completely loyal to your family. You accumulate money from his self-efforts. You earn money with your personality. You receive love and support from family members. You may have spiritual power or intuitive power for good performance in finances. You may have an interest in occult science. Your mind is always indulged with family and is highly possessive with family members.


Moon in Leo in 3rd House
Moon in the third House in Leo represents the brave and intelligent personality of an individual. You are emotional and attached to your brothers and sisters. Your communication or speech supports your siblings. You are highly hard-working with many talents, hobbies and qualities to perform in front of the crowd.

Your emotions are expressed in your communication. You can nurture your siblings. You travel with your family and relish the journey. You accumulate money with self-efforts. You earn money from travelling, communication, food related business, commission, family business etc. In Moon Maha Dasha, your father may change your business.


Moon in Leo in 4th House
Moon in the fourth house in Leo represents your hard-working personality. You acquire money and success in life through diligent effort. You accumulate land, property, vehicles, and all the comforts of life through your own endeavors. Your decision-making abilities are sharp, and you approach choices with cleverness and intelligence.

Emotionally, you share a strong bond with your mother, whose mood is influenced by the situation. You find happiness within your own home. During your Moon Maha Dasha, you may consider changing your residence. Traveling with your mother brings enjoyment, and you might explore business opportunities related to travel or food, such as starting a restaurant or hotel.


Moon in Leo in 5th House
Moon in the fifth house in Leo represents your creative personality. You have a strong emotional connection with your children. Your interests include artistic pursuits, arts, writing, poetry, singing, acting, and sports. Your love life is filled with happiness. This placement also contributes to a good education for you. Chemistry captures your curiosity, enhancing your creative abilities and bringing you recognition, fame, and financial gains through your talents.

Your work reflects passion and expression, and you aspire to excel in your career. The presence of Raj Yog indicates that success comes easily to you during your Moon Maha Dasha. You ensure your children’s comfort by providing them with property, vehicles, and luxury items. However, be prepared for children’s moodiness.


Moon in Leo in 6th House
Moon in the sixth house in Leo may bring a bit of a struggle. You work with logic in life. You may suffer from cold and cough problems. Your education might be related to the medical field and health care. You will care and respect for old women and female guardian figures.

You have the ability to heal others. You spend money on children’s health issues. You provide services in the educational field. You may get a job in arts, education related, children related, creative related, intelligence related etc. In Moon Maha Dasha, the individual gets his job. If the Moon is in bad influence, you may spend money on diseases like cold and cough, loans and debts.


Moon in Leo in 7th House
Moon in the seventh house in Leo brings happy married life. You have the power of attraction among people and will get your job based on skills. You have the skill to maintain a business. You are not satisfied with one business and change business from time to time.

Your partner looks gorgeous, attractive and emotional. If Moon is in bad influence, then it creates conflicts with his partner with issues in marriage life. You get achievements with struggle and compete with enemies with struggle. In your Moon Maha Dasha, it is seen that the individual faces struggle in life and your mother will suffer from health issues.


Moon in Leo in 8th House
Moon in the eighth house in Leo represents the kind and noble personality. You will get success after a lot of difficulties, failure and struggles. You respect the freedom of independence and creative personality of others. You take an interest in the business of serving other people’s needs. You may not want change in life and will be fixed not bending in front of others. That is why you may face marriage problems and business problems.

You may suffer from mental stress and have a hidden fear in mind. Moon is the lord of Marak house so, in the Moon Maha Dasha gives chronic diseases to individuals. It is inauspicious for an individual and brings unfavorable results and creates obstacles in life.


Moon in Leo in 9th House
Moon in the ninth house in Leo represents spiritual personality of individual. He transforms his life into spirituality. He gets higher knowledge of occult science and takes interest in doing research in astrology or occult science. He missed his opportunity due to his unstable luck. He is emotionally attached to his father.

It is seen that the individual may live far away from his father’s or ancestral house. In his Moon Maha Dasha his father has faces some health issues. He earns money from occult science, travelling, commission, freelancing, consultancy etc. He does struggle for material success. He travels to religious and cultural places. He is emotionally attached with ancestral house or place.


Moon in Leo in 10th House
Moon in the tenth house in Leo represents the happy, healthy and wealthy life. You will get status and authority in your career through higher education. You are emotionally attached to a career with a possessive nature in professional life. You may want freedom at work.

You are ambitious and full of pride. You get support from your mother or state government in your career. You have many options for careers like cloth business, navy, hotel, restaurant, NGO etc. In business or company, all employees may be women. It is seen that if Moon is in tenth house the individual changes profession again and again in Moon Maha Dasha.


Moon in Leo in 11th House
Moon in the eleventh house in Leo represents the individual who is lucky and intelligent. You will have multiple sources of income. You are emotionally attached to elder brothers and sisters. You have a good and helpful friend circle. Your friends support you in your career. You fulfill desires and expectations in professional life.

You will achieve professional success in life. You will gain profits from social media, state government, water related business, food related business etc. You will earn profit from the business or company which is in your mother’s name. If the moon is conjunct with malefic planets or aspect by malefic planets in the chart, then the individual wants to fulfill unfavorable and unnecessary desires or expectations. That is why you face problems in life.


Moon in Leo in 12th House
Moon in the twelfth house in Leo represents the individual who is expressive. You will travel long distances or in foreign countries with lots of expenditure. You will get profits from the hospital, healing therapy etc. You earn money from foreign places or far away from your house. You may take an interest in the import-export business.

You believe in charity from your income. If Moon is in bad influence, the individual faces issues with his elder brother or sister. You may spend more than your income. You may suffer from health issues. You may face litigation problems from siblings or spend income on sibling’s health.
