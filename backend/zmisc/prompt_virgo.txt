What happens when Moon combusts in Virgo?
Combusted Moon will create delusion and you will feel insecure about your family, work, finance, and health. Your toxic mind will shake up all areas of your life. With such a position, your decision-making ability gets clouded. It is possible that your mother might abuse you verbally or physically which will create a lot of disturbance and friction in your mind.

 

Moon in Virgo Effects in all the 12 Houses

Moon in Virgo in 1st House
Moon in Virgo will make you brave enough to embrace the challenges and to turn them into new possibilities. More specifically, you must take the lead of your life and inspire others, through your growth and confidence. You will be filled with new ideas and will implement them in a positive way which will bring good luck in your business or profession.

You will value inner spirit, straightforward attitude and high will power. You will be emotional, over sensitive, unpredictable, submissive, and sentimental. You will have a strong desire to achieve a name, fame, and recognition.


Moon in Virgo in 2nd House
You will accumulate wealth, family, speech, and food. You will be charming and influence others through your way of living. You will be the center of attraction through your impressive communication skills. Your head strong and direct approach needs to be balanced to maintain harmony in your personal relationship.

You must include your partner’s valuable input in all major decisions. If not well placed, there might be a few misunderstandings and conflicts in your marital life. You may get scars on your face, headaches, and skin eruptions. It is advised that you avoid oily foods and take a healthy diet. This is a financially weak planetary position.


Moon in Virgo in 3rd house
You are sharp and intellectual and can spot an error with ease and always ensure perfectionism. You believe in making friends and you love following a healthy lifestyle. You keep your environment neat and clean. It is advised that you control your emotions and deal with the situation with your communication skills.

You will notice an increase in your creativity, but this energy might create high expectations with others and can lead to misunderstandings and conflicts. Your spouse will try to make you understand and help you to come out of these illusions. You have inner strength and courage to deal with any situation by creating boundaries.


Moon in Virgo in 4th house
You will be attached to your family and domestic interests. It is usually a challenge for you to concentrate on external affairs. You love your native place and are always ready to do service for the hometown. You are quite concerned about financial security and focus on a realistic and logical approach. You love nurturing and doing service to others.

Furthermore, you will light the fire of passion in your marital life and will cherish even the small moments and turn them into beautiful memories. If not well placed, you will blame your parents for failure and difficulties.


Moon in Virgo in 5th house
This is an excellent placement for prosperity, happiness, and abundance in life. It also brings a lot of blessings and good fortune. You will become a professional artist or sportsperson at a young age. You will gain health and wealth through speculative investments and share markets. You may attain foreign collaborative business and achieve success. If not well placed, it might create confusion in making financial decisions.

You will not have clarity to do financial planning for future aspects. You might experience unforeseen expenses which cannot be avoided. You will not be able to gain profits from your investments and share markets.


Moon in Virgo in 6th house
You will flourish through your knowledge, providing services to others and chasing your dreams. You will be emotionally stable, compassionate and can heal and nurture others. However, you are overcommitted to your responsibilities which can make you a workaholic.

You may also be insecure, which can take a toll on your health and can create stress and anxiety. Due to stress, you might have health problems like high blood pressure or sugar level fluctuation. You will develop a strong relationship with your family. But you may have a rigid and fixated point of view which will not allow you to understand your family’s concern.


Moon in Virgo in 7th house
This placement will bring success, comfort, happiness in your Married and romantic life. However, you will be overprotective and possessive about your partner which can bring unhappiness in the relationship. Probably, you may go through heart breaks and short-lived relationships. If not well placed, this will disturb the family’s harmony and there will be disagreements and communication problems.

Try to take the initiative at the workplace and do not make any aggressive decisions because chances are that you might face challenges because of confusion. Work irregularities may require you to redo your tasks.


Moon in Virgo in 8th house
You are determined and focused to achieve your goals. You will acquire knowledge from multiple fields. You will be calm and polite in most challenging situations, and this is an advantage for you. You are honest and trustworthy and will pursue healing, counselling, spiritual leadership, and a nurturing field where people need your help. If afflicted, relationships will demand space, and your rigid approach and negative thoughts can bring challenges.

There might be delays in getting the expected results on the professional front because of lack of support or unclear communication with others. Miscommunication can create arguments with family members, so try to adopt a polite approach and healthy conversations.


Moon in Virgo in 9th house
You will achieve success in mid age, around 30 to 40. You may become a philosopher or a life coach. Sudden opportunities far from your present place can be presented and an inclination towards spirituality is present from early stages of your life. Foreign relationships through partners, business, and friends will be beneficial for you. You will achieve higher education and will be more creative than others. If not well placed, you will be disconnected from others and will be restless.

The natives of Moon in the 9th House are very restless and can get disconnected from their purpose if they don’t have any specific goal. Also, the natives may long to be elsewhere if they are unhappy. This dissatisfaction can help them improve the situation so that they convert their flaws into growth and view it as a development opportunity.


Moon in Virgo in 10th house
You are always curious to learn new things and focus on being creative in your career. You often switch your job if things are not profitable for you. You believe in doing smart work rather than hard work. You are not as home centric and will focus more on your career and public life.

You will achieve happiness and will get support from your partner. But if afflicted, you will face discrepancies in the relationship. This planetary placement will elevate your aggressiveness and will trigger impulsive decisions in your love life. You will be quite attracted towards your partner and increase your demands and expectations from them.


Moon in Virgo in 11th house
You will love to connect with people to increase your social network. You will always get support from your family, relatives, and well-wishers. Financial gains and support will be present from your family members and loved ones. You are emotionally stable when you socialize with everyone. Your desires will be fulfilled, and you will earn profits and gains. If afflicted, you become vulnerable and weak, and you will face challenges.

You will be able to make the expected savings with the support of your family. Rivals and enemies will not be able to harm you, but unnecessary fear or stress needs to be replaced with a positive approach.


Moon in Virgo in 12th House
You will be imaginative, kind and a nature lover. You may have regular emotional ups and downs and will seek constant attention. You will be extremely talented, and power packed with creativity. You will notice small things and provide happiness and care for your family members, and this is how you nurture everyone. But things become tricky when you expect the same from others.

You will have many hidden enemies who will take advantage of you and might backstab you. But you believe in liberating people through forgiveness. If afflicted, you will be in agony and take revenge from enemies. You may also feel a lack of nourishment from your parents.
